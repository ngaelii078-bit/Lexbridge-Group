## Claims that do not ship without proof
"1000's+ satisfied clients", "100's+ trusted partners", "50+ industries" (the profile lists 10), "1000's of containers", "on-time, every time", "zero compromise", "leading", "industry leaders", "strongest partners". Unverifiable claims are a Consumer Protection Act risk and procurement managers discount them.
- Customs: write "coordinates through specialist partners", never "we clear".
- "Independent": needs the answer to how LexBridge is paid, printed on the page.
- Headline overlap: OneLogix already runs "One logistics partner for your entire supply chain" over its own truck. The line survives only if the page proves independence. If the client confirms owning no fleet, say so near the top.

## Usable third-party proof (not a LexBridge result, label it)
Kiggell, Kilbourn and Heyns, 2021, University of Johannesburg, Journal of Transport and Supply Chain Management 15, 10.4102/jtscm.v15i0.606. A large SA non-food retailer handed courier management to a fourth-party provider. The provider "committed to returning a saving of 25% in the first year"; the abstract says targets were exceeded and savings were "consistent over a 10-year period". Single case study. Other figures were read in summary only: check the paper before printing them.
