## Step 3: Intent (the bakeoff rubric)

An owner, operations manager or procurement manager at an established South African business, arriving from a referral or a direct message with three courier accounts and one unexplained freight invoice open in other tabs, should feel **relief** within the first two screens, because the only thing we want is fifteen minutes of their time, and people hand a problem over when they believe the searching is already done. Urgency or excitement would send them off to collect quotes and compare. The feeling we are displacing is the wariness of being one more account number: OneLogix shows a truck and asks for a quote, CourierIT asks for a signup, and Bob Go hands over software and leaves the work with the visitor.

How the family delivers it: in all five, something scattered becomes one thing and then stops moving. Relief is the stop.

Signature move: deliberately not locked across families. The FedPump note says a move locked before the bakeoff stops three of the four criteria discriminating. The winner's move becomes the signature.
